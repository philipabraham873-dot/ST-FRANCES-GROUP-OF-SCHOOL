About Us

About Us

Omolere Nursery and Primary School is a coeducational institution established by a seasoned educationalist. The Proprietor late Chief (Mrs) Frances Oladiwura Fasoranti (Nee Awosika) attended the prestigious UMC Unified Missionary College Ibadan and later proceeded to the University of Liverpool where she obtained a degree in education. She worked briefly with the Ministry of Education in the old western region as an Inspector of Education, later resigned her appointment and founded Omolere Nursery and Primary Akure on 16th January, 1967.

Location

The school is located in the suburb of Akure town, Ondo State capital of Nigeria. The school is situated on No 23, Fasoranti Avenue, Akure with its annex at Igoba, Akure, Ondo State.

Aims and Objectives

The aims and objectives of the school are in line with the National Policy on Education. The institution provides comprehensive education for the Kindergarten class, Nursery and Primary School pupils. It also prepares pupils for sound foundation for higher education.

Omolere History

Founding Date: January 16, 1967

Founder: Chief (Mrs.) Frances Oladiwura Fasoranti, an educationist who studied at UMC (Unified Memorial College) Ibadan and the University of Liverpool, alongside her husband, the elder statesman and Afenifere leader Pa Reuben Famuyide Fasoranti. Background: Chief (Mrs.) Fasoranti worked briefly as an education inspector with the Ministry of Education in the old Western Region before resigning to establish the institution Location and Development Address: Situated at No. 21 (or 23) Fasoranti Avenue, Akure, Ondo State.Growth: The co-educational institution grew to become one of the oldest and most famous elementary schools in Akure. The success of Omolere also inspired the founders later to establish other notable institutions, such as Akure High School.

Why Families Choose Us

Many of the students who begin at Omolere as toddlers go on to St. Frances' Academy for secondary school,continuing the same standard of care under one school family. For us, that continuity isn't a coincidence. It's the whole point.
