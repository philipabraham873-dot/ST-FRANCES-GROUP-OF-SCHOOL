A WORD FROM THE FOUNDERS

Add a photo of the Proprietor/Chairman here

Dear Parents, Guardians, Students, and Friends of St. Frances' Academy Group, It is with great pride and gratitude that we welcome you to the St. Frances' Academy Group, a family of schools built on a single vision: that every child, from their very first day in nursery to the day they leave secondary school, deserves an education that forms both mind and character. When our journey began, it started with a shared conviction that quality education should not be a privilege reserved for a few, but a standard we work to bring within reach of every family we serve. Over the years, that conviction has grown into two schools, each with its own identity and leadership, yet united by the same values we set out with: discipline, excellence, integrity, and genuine care for every child who passes through our doors. We are deeply grateful to the proprietors, head teachers, principals, and staff across both schools, whose daily dedication brings our vision to life. And we are equally grateful to you, our parents and guardians, for trusting us with the education and formation of your children. As the Group continues to grow, our commitment remains the same as it was on day one: to raise young people who are academically sound, morally grounded, and ready to make a meaningful impact on the world around them. Thank you for being part of this journey with us. With deep appreciation,

Chief (Pa) Ruben Famuyide Fasoranti and , Chief(Mrs) Frances Fasoranti, FOUNDERS

Why Families Choose Us

Whichever school your child starts in, they're joining one group with one shared standard, not two unrelated institutions under the same roof.

Continuity from Nursery to SSS3

A child can move straight from their first day at Omolere through to graduation from St. Frances' Academy, without changing school families.

Day and Boarding Flexibility

Families choose the arrangement that fits their household in either section, with the same consistent care whichever option they pick.

One Family, One Standard of Care

Omolere and St. Frances' share a single proprietorship and set of values, so what parents trust at one age holds true at the next.
