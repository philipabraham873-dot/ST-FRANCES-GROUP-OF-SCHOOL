A Welcome Address From Our Cordinatorl

As an offshoot of Omolere Nursery/Primary School, we carry forward the same values of discipline, care, and academic excellence, now shaped for the secondary school years. This is the stage where young people begin to find their direction, and our role is to guide that process with structure, patience, and high expectations. Our students are prepared not just to pass WAEC and NECO, but to think clearly, work hard, and carry themselves with confidence beyond the classroom. We run a full academic programme alongside clubs, sports, and leadership opportunities, because we believe a well rounded student performs better in every area of life. We maintain close attention to each student's progress, both Day and Boarding, and we work in partnership with parents every step of the way. Our doors are always open to you, and we welcome you to visit the campus and see our approach firsthand. Thank you for trusting St. Frances' Academy with your child's secondary education.

ADEYEMI LOAYEMI JOSEPHINE
CORDINATOR
